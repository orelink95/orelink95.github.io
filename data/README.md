# Site data

> **These files are the only copy of the site's places, people, library and campaigns.**
> Edit them with the dashboard: open `admin_tools/dashboard.html` in Chrome or Edge (double-clicking the file works), link the site's root folder once, edit, then **Save** (Ctrl+S). It refuses to save while a link is broken. You can also edit the JSON by hand and check it with `node tools/validate.js`.

One file per type. Every record has an `id` (never renamed), a `name` and optional `aliases`.
`data/schema.js` declares every field; `node tools/validate.js` checks the files against it.

- **Links are ids.** Write `"home": "gilded-goldfinch"`, never a display name. Lists such as a POI's residents or a faction's members are worked out by the site.
- **Map pins live on the child.** `map: {x, y, icon}` places a record on its parent's map. Only continents have a `layer` (the layer buttons of the world map). A settlement has no map of its own (its maps are its districts'), so a POI sitting directly in a settlement has no pin.
- **Long text is Markdown.** Blank line = new paragraph; `**bold**`, `*italic*`, `[text](url)`, `> quote` with `> — Name` on the last line.
- **A tavern is a POI with a `tavern` block** (tier, picks, signature items, services). Its staff are the NPCs whose `home` is that POI. Its page is `poi.html?id=<poi id>`.
- **Tavern menus change with `tavernCode`** in `settings.json` (the market code): same code, same menu, same wares and same rumour order for every player. Preview another code with `poi.html?id=…&code=XYZ` (not saved).
- **One POI, several roles.** A POI can have a `tavern`, a `shop` and a `temple` block at once (a tavern that is also a general store: fill in both). Each role adds a tab to its page — Fare, Wares, Shrine, Rumours, Board — and on phones the tabbed card comes right under the picture, its tabs stuck under the header. `poi.html?id=…#wares` opens a tab.
- **Shops.** `shop.kind` sets the shelves (`shopCategory`, 35 of them, from Breads to Funerary Goods and Trades & Services). There are 33 kinds: food (bakery, pastry shop, sweet shop, grocer, butcher, fishmonger, cheesemonger, spice & tea merchant, wine merchant, market stall), clothes and finery (tailor, draper, cobbler, jeweller, perfumer, barber-surgeon), arms (smithy, bowyer), craft and home (potter, chandler, florist, toymaker, instrument maker, tobacconist, pet shop), learning and magic (alchemist, arcane emporium, scribe, curiosity shop), plus general store, stable, undertaker and fence. A kind's `shelfTags` narrows a shelf to goods with one of its tags: a barber's Trades & Services shelf shows only goods tagged `barber`. Kinds and shelves are edited in `data/schema.js` (`enums`); each shelf shows `picks` seeded goods (default 5) from `shop-goods.json` that the tier and tags allow, plus the shop's `signature` wares. `keepers` names the shopkeeper(s), shown at the top of the Wares tab: any NPC, so a POI with several residents (a tavern that is also a store) can say who minds the shop. Taverns (`tavern.keepers`, “Behind the bar”) and temples (`temple.keepers`, “Tending the altar”) have the same field. Price = `basePrice` × tier multiplier × `markup`%. With no `tier`, a shop takes its tavern's. Goods tagged `illicit` are only sold by fences (or a shop whose `includeTags` has `illicit`).
- **Temples.** `temple.deities` (Deity records, shown in the Pantheon, `pantheon.html`), `services` from `temple-services.json` (prices × the temple's tier) and `customServices` (no price = “Donation”). “Pray” draws one blessing at random from `blessings.json` (those with no `deities`, or one of this temple's); each player gets one per temple until the market code changes. `noPrayer` hides the button.
- **The Pantheon.** `pantheon.html` shows every god as a card, one section per `tier` (The Creators, The Great Pantheon, Minor Gods, Primordials, Elder Gods: the `deityTier` enum, in page order); a card opens the god's page, `deity.html?id=…` (old `pantheon.html?id=…` links still work). Each tier has its own frame (`scripts/deity-card.js`, `styles/deity-card.css`): give a god a `portrait` (full body, upright 2:3; `portraitZoom` zooms into it, in %, centred on `portraitFocus`, e.g. `50% 20%`; with no zoom the whole portrait shows), a `symbol` (a PNG with a transparent background, shown in the medallion) and a `colour` (the gems and the glow). `artifact` is an item: the god's page tells it as a legend (`artifactLegend`), and it reaches the Vault only once a session's loot or a party's inventory lists it, like any item; until then its name is not a keyword link either. **Elder gods are sealed**: a nameless slot in the Pantheon, a sealed page, no keyword link, left out of their temples, until their `revealedIn` lists the campaign. The Pantheon has the campaign drop-down of Dramatis Personae; other pages follow the campaign last picked in the browser tab (else `currentCampaign`). In the dashboard, a god's side panel previews its card (Revealed / Sealed for an Elder god) and lists, campaign by campaign, where it is revealed.
- **Rumours.** A rumour's `where` can be a POI (that tavern only) or any place above it (a settlement's rumours are heard in all its taverns). A tavern tells its rumours in a seeded order (per market code); each player hears the first `rumourDraws` (default 3) that belong to their campaign, one per “Buy the room a round”. Their draws are kept in their browser and start again when the code changes. `truth` and `note` are DM-only (`private.json`). In the dashboard, a tavern's side panel lists its rumours in order (✓ = heard in the current campaign).
- **Gaming table.** Tick games in a tavern's `tavern.games` (Liar's Dice, Knucklebones) to give it a Games tab and a free “Gaming table” line in its services. Players play in the browser against bots: the NPCs whose home is the tavern first, then generic regulars. Nothing is saved but a won/lost tally in each player's browser. To add a game: a file in `scripts/games/` that calls `Athenaeum.games.register()` (see `scripts/games/table.js`), loaded by `poi.html`, plus its id in the `tavernGame` enum.
- **Lists with an `enum`** in the schema (tavern games, shop shelves) are ticked as checkboxes in the dashboard; the validator rejects ids not in the enum.
- **Notice boards.** A notice's `where` is a settlement (its Town Board, under the map) or a POI (its Board tab). `kind` sets the look (wanted posters have a picture), `status` stamps it Taken or Closed and sends it to the end. Like NPCs, `campaigns` (none = every campaign) decides who sees it; the POI and settlement pages share the campaign drop-down of Dramatis Personae. A POI's or settlement's side panel in the dashboard has **+ Rumour** / **+ Notice** buttons that create one already pinned there.
- **NPCs somewhere off the map** (another plane…) have no `home`; put a line of text in `whereabouts` instead.
- **Keywords.** Names of NPCs, places, factions and creatures become links on their own. A term with no page (a war, a lost civilisation) is a glossary term in `lore.json`; its `kind` sets the colour and its `definition` the tooltip in the scriptorium. Give an NPC, faction or place a `summary` to show a tooltip for it too.
- **Hero secrets** are written in `private.json` (`secret` and `password`). The dashboard encrypts them into `sealedSecret` when it saves, so the live site shows a secret only to someone who types its password.
- **Loot cards.** Every item is a card in the Artifact Vault (`vault.html`). Players see the items listed in their campaign's sessions (`loot`) and the party's own (`inventory`). Fill in `type`, `rarity` (sets the card's colour), `cost`, `weight`, `attunement`, `attunedBy`, a one-line `summary` for the front and a full `body` for the opened card. Items from the newest session lie face down until a player turns them over. In the dashboard, **+ New** on Items asks where the party found it (a session's loot or a party's inventory) and the side panel previews the card, front and opened. The card's markup and styles are shared: `scripts/loot-card.js` and `styles/loot-card.css`.
- **Spell cards.** Every spell is a card in the Book of Shadows (`spellbook.html`): pick a campaign, then a hero. What a hero knows is written on the **campaign**, in `spellbooks` (one entry per hero: `hero` + `spells`), so a hero in two campaigns can know different spells in each. Fill in `level` (0 = cantrip, shown as C), `school` (sets the card's colour and symbols), `castingTime`, `range`, `duration`, the `concentration`, `ritual`, `verbal`, `somatic` and `material` boxes, `materials` (the components: shown when hovering the M, which is then marked with a star), a one-line `summary` for the card and a full `body` for the opened card. The same cards open from a hero's card on `heroes.html`. A spell a browser has never turned over lies face down (its back takes the school's colour); the first click turns it, the next opens it. In the dashboard, the side panel previews the card, its back and the opened card. Markup and styles: `scripts/spell-card.js` and `styles/spell-card.css`; school symbols and the back's art (`card-back.png`) in `images/spells/`.
- **Upcoming sessions** (homepage cards) are `sessions` in `schedule.json`, shown in file order: `campaign`, `number`, `date` (YYYY-MM-DD), `time` (HH:MM), optional `status` (`tentative` = purple TBD card, `postponed` = amber with the date struck through, `cancelled` = red and dimmed; none = confirmed), `notice` (a message on the card), `title`, `where`, `link`. A date that has passed is hidden on its own; the first confirmed card still to come is “Up Next”, with a countdown. Confirmed cards have an **Add to calendar** button (an .ics event lasting `length` hours, default 4, with a reminder 2 hours before). In the dashboard (Site › Upcoming sessions), **+ Add a session** prefills the next number, date and time; the side panel previews the homepage and can sort by date or remove past sessions.
- **NPCs and factions per campaign.** An NPC lists its campaigns in `campaigns` (one entry per campaign: `campaign` + the party's `relation` there), so the same NPC can be an ally in one campaign and hostile in another. A faction belongs to the campaigns whose `standings` list it (`faction` + `status`). One listed in no campaign is part of every campaign, with its own `relation` (NPC) or `status` (faction). `npc.html`, `heroes.html` and `factions.html` share one campaign drop-down (`scripts/personae.js`); it opens on `currentCampaign` from `settings.json`, and NPCs and Factions also offer *All campaigns*. Pages ask the store: `store.inCampaign(record, campaignId)` and `store.standing(record, campaignId)`.
- **Drafts.** A tome or handout with `"draft": true` stays off the site.
- **Check your edits** with `node tools/validate.js` (broken links, unknown values, missing images and map icons).
- **The dashboard's forms come from the schema.** Each field's `label` (short), `help`, `section` (the card it sits in; a type lists its cards in `sections`), `width` and `showIf` (shown only when another field has a value) are read by the dashboard only. Shortcuts: **Ctrl K** finds any record, **Alt ↑ / ↓** opens the previous / next one, **Ctrl S** saves. A gold dot marks what is not saved yet.
